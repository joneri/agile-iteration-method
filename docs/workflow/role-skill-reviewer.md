# Reviewer engineering skill

Skill id: `aim-reviewer-engineering`. Bundled with AIM; applies to review.

Begin with the intended user's task and the working product. Use its normal
entry or consumer route to judge usefulness, behavior, clarity, interaction and
relevant experience. Tests support that judgment; passing commands and complete
paperwork cannot establish product quality.

Before a new material plan is frozen, derive the few decisive expectations from
the original request, agreed context and applicable domain references, then
challenge the author's proposal. Separate explicit outcomes, supported implied
core, uncertain assumptions and optional preferences. Missing wording alone is
not optionality. Link consequential inferences to a brief reference/observation,
omission consequence and observable check. Preserve explicit simplifications;
references do not authorize a larger feature inventory. Use the proportional
contract in `engineering-delivery.md` and retain this in existing evidence.

For material work, begin one continuing independent review at the first usable
core before costly dependent expansion. Correct material in-scope gaps and
recheck affected behavior. Carry credible observations forward. Final integrated
review judges the delivered whole, changes and consequential remaining uncertainty;
it does not restart verification because the phase changed. Preserve the normal
readiness record and actual owner acceptance. Nonmaterial work keeps its justified
self-review policy. Early user access or feedback adds no gate or acceptance.

Compare the result and decomposition with original intent, selected criteria
and the agreed Epic contribution. Do not accept a narrow implementation's own
tests as its completeness reference. Identify affected Story IDs in findings.
An unrequested bound that excludes required use is a product gap. Preserve
requirements, justified quality targets and source/instance identity.

Choose the smallest sufficient observation for the remaining risk. Reuse current
independent evidence and existing checks. Direct use can suffice; do not require
a new automated harness, user-route map, exhaustive successful journey or fresh
counterexample without a consequential gap. Controlled boundary states establish
only the behavior they exercise. A real defect merits correction and a targeted
regression when useful. Stop expanding checks when they are unlikely to improve
the product or change readiness; required project/release checks still apply.

Inspect relevant source and actual observations, including rendered behavior and
side effects. Missing credible evidence for decisive binding behavior remains an
open material readiness finding; uncertainty about an optional or already
sufficiently covered route is not automatically a product defect. Do not hide
central usability failures behind technical passes or call them taste. Keep
preference-only findings advisory. Review copy in its actual context for purpose,
clarity, supported claims and accessibility.

Use domain checks only where they address the changed behavior or a concrete
unresolved risk. For algorithms/state logic, use independent expected results
and representative consequential variants. For substantial computation, inspect
correct complete results on comparable workloads and actual numeric targets;
responsiveness alone is insufficient. For input/async/mutation/security boundaries,
choose the relevant rejection, stale-result, committed/unknown-outcome or next
consumer-action case. No universal probe list, fault injection or benchmark
campaign is required. A hash proves bytes, not execution, semantic coverage or
identity. Preserve exact frozen observation mappings and numeric comparability.

Recall shared lessons from the actual changed behavior before material review;
reuse a relevant unchanged planning answer. Advice supplies no proof or authority.
For AIM method-learning work, consume the existing selected triage record and
check product remedy and method cause separately; retain uncertainty and actual
pre-readiness detections. Cross-check changed documentation/native loaders against
current behavior. Reuse the entry and Story contract; read companion sections
only for an unresolved ownership, resource, authority or recovery question.

Report reproducible findings with severity, affected obligation and sufficient
evidence in the existing review. Keep inspection read-only except isolated test
artifacts; do not accept work or mutate runtime. Re-review affected corrections,
not every passed fact. For early read inputs, preserved original baselines and
verified identical-byte relocations remain distinct from current output proof.

Prepare the original version-1 report with `aim_stories.py review-record` and the
coordinator's confirmed review receipt digest when saving the actual assessment.
Supply `deliveryId`, actual `implementer`/`implementers` and `reviewer` id/session
pairs, `reviewMode` and explicit `findings` (id, material/advisory severity,
open/resolved status, description). Retain observations/counterexamples there.
The helper supplies frozen source bindings/scope; check success before writing
and preserve prior evidence on failure. The first `validate.evidence` reference
consumes that report without another eligibility round. Inspect the complete
changed set and relevant dependencies; declared identities do not authenticate
separation. Justified self-review never becomes independent review.
