AIM 4.0
Start with people. Deliver what matters.

Suggested announcement copy · AIM 4.0 · 12 October 2026.

AI can help us build more software. AIM starts with a different question: whose problem are we solving?

AIM 4.0 puts User Stories at the heart of AI-assisted development. An intent can reveal several Epics and several people with different needs. Those needs become Stories with clear value and acceptance criteria, prioritized together rather than confined to one Epic at a time.

The Product Owner owns the requirements and priorities. The Technical Delivery Owner helps refine the Stories and selects a coherent delivery. Developers implement it, a separate Reviewer checks it, and validation connects evidence to the criteria. The user accepts the result.

In AIM UI, Stories are the cards that move across the board. Epics keep the bigger outcomes visible. A delivery can include Stories from different Epics; an increment is the working improvement it produces. Done means accepted behaviour.

For developers and product teams using Codex, GitHub Copilot or Claude Code, AIM offers a shared method for turning an idea into software people can evaluate and accept.
