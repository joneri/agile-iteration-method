AIM
Build your product from an intent.

Suggested announcement copy · AIM 4.6 · 18 October 2026.

Describe the software you want in plain language. AIM turns that goal into User Stories and a coherent plan, with agents configured for your project. Follow development in AIM UI, inspect the review and checks, and accept the working result. AIM Graph shows what can start next, what blocks delivery and which evidence needs updating.

Ship is the default: complete the agreed product before final acceptance. Choose Loop for useful Done Increments with recurring owner feedback. Graph engineering makes ownership, dependencies and proof explicit; an agent owner needs your explicit mandate to decide in Strict Loop.

AIM agrees quality goals before implementation. A separate reviewer challenges the first working core before costly expansion. Real user journeys and retained raw results support the final assessment. A maintained verification route helps later changes repeat the right checks.

Repository knowledge, compact checkpoints and relevant shared lessons help agents reuse work that still applies. Learning is part of ordinary delivery, without a manual reflection step. The Product Owner owns Epic outcomes and priority; the Technical Delivery Owner owns Stories, criteria and the increment. Acceptance stays with you.

The retained Enigma M3 results compare GPT-6 Luna with AIM, scoring 96.5/100 in the latest assessment, with GPT-6 Luna without AIM at 49.8/100. The earlier AIM result was 93.5/100. The same model, high reasoning effort, brief and scoring weights were used, but the runs took place on different dates under different delivery conditions. This is a historical comparison, not a fresh paired experiment or a general quality or cost guarantee. The latest version of AIM includes later changes outside this score; the assessed native delivery remained unfinished. Exact versions and evidence are in the evaluation report.
