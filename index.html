US-01 · Customer recovery

Try a failed payment again

As a customer, I need to retry a failed payment so that I can complete my purchase without starting over.

Observable acceptance criteria

  • A retry keeps the existing order and its items.
  • A successful retry creates one payment, without a duplicate charge.

A clearer path from
intent to impact.

You bring the problem or ambition. AIM helps shape the requirements, plan the work, and check what was actually delivered.

01

Intent

The change you want to make.

02

Epics

The outcomes worth pursuing.

03

User Stories

People’s needs, prioritized independently.

04

Working increments

Useful improvements with evidence.

A Story is a requirement. An increment is a working product improvement. A Sprint, if you use one, is a timebox. Each has its own purpose.

Acceptance stays with you. Selected Stories keep their criteria and evidence. Accepting a delivery does not automatically close its Epics.

PO

Own the need

Clarify outcomes, user perspectives and priority.

TDO

Plan a coherent delivery

Refine with PO and select Stories across Epics.

Dev

Reviewer

Challenge the result

Check correctness and evidence before validation and your acceptance.

Already using AIM?

Use /aim upgrade to refresh AIM-owned files. Existing delivery history is preserved; legacy work is not silently turned into Stories.

Calibrate with /aim calibrate-repo, retain useful knowledge with /aim remember-repo, and examine lessons with /aim reflect or /aim reflect-all. Explore reflection.
